Notes on this pass

    Fueling targets for your goal

    The VE and MAF corrections make the engine hit whatever the ECU commands. Set these targets in your Power Enrichment and base fueling tables so it commands the right mixture for your goal.

    Putting it back into VCM Editor

      Check every change before you flash it. Run several logging passes with small changes rather than one big one, tune wide-open throttle only with a wideband sensor, and watch knock retard on every pull. This tool only ever removes spark timing; adding timing safely needs a dyno or an experienced tuner. Not affiliated with HP Tuners.
